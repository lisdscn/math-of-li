
# 4.3 曲面的结构方程

任何光滑函数$f$的二阶偏导数可交换求导次序，即

$$
\frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial u^\alpha \partial u^\beta} = \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial u^\beta \partial u^\alpha}
$$

而这对$\boldsymbol{r},\boldsymbol{r}_\alpha,\boldsymbol{n}$同样适用，因此我们有

$$
\frac{\partial}{\partial u^\beta}\left(\frac{\partial \boldsymbol{r}}{\partial u^\alpha}\right) = \frac{\partial}{\partial u^\alpha}\left(\frac{\partial \boldsymbol{r}}{\partial u^\beta}\right) \tag{3.1}
$$

$$
\frac{\partial}{\partial u^\gamma}\left(\frac{\partial \boldsymbol{r}_\alpha}{\partial u^\beta}\right) = \frac{\partial}{\partial u^\beta}\left(\frac{\partial \boldsymbol{r}_\alpha}{\partial u^\gamma}\right) \tag{3.2}
$$

$$
\frac{\partial}{\partial u^\beta}\left(\frac{\partial \boldsymbol{n}}{\partial u^\alpha}\right) = \frac{\partial}{\partial u^\alpha}\left(\frac{\partial \boldsymbol{n}}{\partial u^\beta}\right) \tag{3.3}
$$

$\boldsymbol{r},\boldsymbol{r}_\alpha,\boldsymbol{n}$满足运动方程


$$
\begin{align}
\frac{\partial \boldsymbol{r}}{\partial u^\alpha} &= \boldsymbol{r}_\alpha,\quad \alpha=1,2; \tag*{($M_1$)} \\[4pt]
\frac{\partial \boldsymbol{r}_\alpha}{\partial u^\beta} &= \Gamma_{\alpha\beta}^\gamma \boldsymbol{r}_\gamma + b_{\alpha\beta}\boldsymbol{n},\quad \alpha,\beta=1,2; \tag*{($M_2$)} \\[4pt]
\frac{\partial \boldsymbol{n}}{\partial u^\alpha} &= -b_\alpha^\beta \boldsymbol{r}_\beta,\quad \beta=1,2; \tag*{($M_3$)}
\end{align}
$$

将$(M_1)$代入$(3.1)$得：$\boldsymbol{r}_{\alpha\beta}=\boldsymbol{r}_{\beta\alpha}$。

将$(M_2)$代入$(3.2)$，有

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\partial}{\partial u^\gamma}\left(\frac{\partial \boldsymbol{r}_\alpha}{\partial u^\beta}\right)
&= \frac{\partial}{\partial u^\gamma}\big(\Gamma_{\alpha\beta}^\varepsilon \boldsymbol{r}_\varepsilon + b_{\alpha\beta}\boldsymbol{n}\big) \\
&= \frac{\partial \Gamma_{\alpha\beta}^\varepsilon}{\partial u^\gamma}\boldsymbol{r}_\varepsilon + \Gamma_{\alpha\beta}^\varepsilon \boldsymbol{r}_{\varepsilon\gamma} + \frac{\partial b_{\alpha\beta}}{\partial u^\gamma}\boldsymbol{n} + b_{\alpha\beta}\boldsymbol{n}_\gamma \tag{3.4}
\end{aligned}
$$

再利用$(M_2)$和$(M_3)$就有

$$
\frac{\partial}{\partial u^\gamma}\left(\frac{\partial \boldsymbol{r}_\alpha}{\partial u^\beta}\right)
= \left(\frac{\partial \Gamma_{\alpha\beta}^\varepsilon}{\partial u^\gamma}+\Gamma_{\alpha\beta}^\eta \Gamma_{\eta\gamma}^\varepsilon -b_{\alpha\beta}b_\gamma^\varepsilon\right)\boldsymbol{r}_\varepsilon
+\left(\Gamma_{\alpha\beta}^\varepsilon b_{\varepsilon\gamma}+\frac{\partial b_{\alpha\beta}}{\partial u^\gamma}\right)\boldsymbol{n}. \tag{3.5}
$$

同理，将运动方程代入$(3.2)$式有

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\partial}{\partial u^\beta}\left(\frac{\partial \boldsymbol{r}_\alpha}{\partial u^\gamma}\right)
&= \frac{\partial \Gamma_{\alpha\gamma}^\varepsilon}{\partial u^\beta}\boldsymbol{r}_\varepsilon + \Gamma_{\alpha\gamma}^\varepsilon\big(\Gamma_{\varepsilon\beta}^\eta \boldsymbol{r}_\eta +b_{\varepsilon\beta}\boldsymbol{n}\big)+\frac{\partial b_{\alpha\gamma}}{\partial u^\beta}\boldsymbol{n}+b_{\alpha\gamma}(-b_\beta^\xi \boldsymbol{r}_\xi) \\
&= \left(\frac{\partial \Gamma_{\alpha\gamma}^\varepsilon}{\partial u^\beta}+\Gamma_{\alpha\gamma}^\eta \Gamma_{\eta\beta}^\varepsilon -b_{\alpha\gamma}b_\beta^\varepsilon\right)\boldsymbol{r}_\varepsilon
+\left(\Gamma_{\alpha\gamma}^\varepsilon b_{\varepsilon\beta}+\frac{\partial b_{\alpha\gamma}}{\partial u^\beta}\right)\boldsymbol{n} \tag{3.6}
\end{aligned}
$$

由$(3.5)$与$(3.6)$等式相同，有

$$
\frac{\partial \Gamma_{\alpha\beta}^\varepsilon}{\partial u^\gamma}-\frac{\partial \Gamma_{\alpha\gamma}^\varepsilon}{\partial u^\beta}
+\Gamma_{\alpha\beta}^\eta \Gamma_{\eta\gamma}^\varepsilon-\Gamma_{\alpha\gamma}^\eta \Gamma_{\eta\beta}^\varepsilon
-b_{\alpha\beta}b_\gamma^\varepsilon+b_{\alpha\gamma}b_\beta^\varepsilon =0, \tag{3.7}
$$

$$
\frac{\partial b_{\alpha\beta}}{\partial u^\gamma}-\frac{\partial b_{\alpha\gamma}}{\partial u^\beta}
+\Gamma_{\alpha\beta}^\varepsilon b_{\varepsilon\gamma}-\Gamma_{\alpha\gamma}^\varepsilon b_{\varepsilon\beta}=0 \tag{3.8}
$$

把$(M_3)$代入$(3.5)$式有

$$
\frac{\partial b_\beta^\varepsilon}{\partial u^\gamma}-\frac{\partial b_\gamma^\varepsilon}{\partial u^\beta}
=-b_\beta^\eta \Gamma_{\eta\gamma}^\varepsilon +b_\gamma^\eta \Gamma_{\eta\beta}^\varepsilon \tag{3.9}
$$

利用 $b_\alpha^\beta = b_{\alpha\gamma}g^{\gamma\beta}$，可证$(3.8)$与$(3.9)$相互等价。

方程$(3.7)$为曲面的Guass方程；方程$(3.9)$为曲面的Codazzi方程，合称为Guass–Codazzi方程或曲面的结构方程。

由于$\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\varepsilon=1,2$; Guass–Codazzi方程形式上复杂。
引入Riemann记号：

$$
R_{\delta\alpha\beta\gamma}=g_{\delta\varepsilon}\left(
\frac{\partial \Gamma_{\alpha\beta}^\varepsilon}{\partial u^\gamma}-\frac{\partial \Gamma_{\alpha\gamma}^\varepsilon}{\partial u^\beta}
+\Gamma_{\alpha\beta}^\eta \Gamma_{\eta\gamma}^\varepsilon-\Gamma_{\alpha\gamma}^\eta \Gamma_{\eta\beta}^\varepsilon
\right) \tag{3.10}
$$

Guass方程$(3.7)$可改为

$$
R_{\delta\alpha\beta\gamma}=g_{\delta\varepsilon}\big(b_{\alpha\beta}b_\gamma^\varepsilon-b_{\alpha\gamma}b_\beta^\varepsilon\big)
=-\big(b_{\alpha\gamma}b_{\beta\delta}-b_{\alpha\beta}b_{\gamma\delta}\big) \tag{3.11}
$$

利用第二类Christoffel符号$\Gamma_{\alpha\beta}^\varepsilon =g_{\varepsilon\xi}\Gamma_{\alpha\beta\xi}$，由$(3.10)$可知

$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{\delta\alpha\beta\gamma}
&= \frac{\partial \Gamma_{\delta\alpha\beta}}{\partial u^\gamma}-\frac{\partial \Gamma_{\delta\alpha\gamma}}{\partial u^\beta}
-\frac{\partial g_{\delta\eta}}{\partial u^\gamma}\Gamma_{\alpha\beta}^\eta+\frac{\partial g_{\delta\eta}}{\partial u^\beta}\Gamma_{\alpha\gamma}^\eta
+\Gamma_{\alpha\beta}^\eta \Gamma_{\delta\eta\gamma}-\Gamma_{\alpha\gamma}^\eta \Gamma_{\delta\eta\beta} \\
&= \frac{\partial \Gamma_{\delta\alpha\beta}}{\partial u^\gamma}-\frac{\partial \Gamma_{\delta\alpha\gamma}}{\partial u^\beta}
+\Gamma_{\alpha\beta}^\eta\left(\Gamma_{\delta\eta\gamma}-\frac{\partial g_{\delta\eta}}{\partial u^\gamma}\right)
-\Gamma_{\alpha\gamma}^\eta\left(\Gamma_{\delta\eta\beta}-\frac{\partial g_{\delta\eta}}{\partial u^\beta}\right)
\end{aligned} \tag{3.12}
$$

故

$$
R_{\delta\alpha\beta\gamma}=
\frac12\left(
\frac{\partial^2g_{\delta\beta}}{\partial u^\gamma\partial u^\alpha}
-\frac{\partial^2g_{\alpha\beta}}{\partial u^\delta\partial u^\gamma}
-\frac{\partial^2g_{\delta\gamma}}{\partial u^\alpha\partial u^\beta}
+\frac{\partial^2g_{\alpha\gamma}}{\partial u^\beta\partial u^\delta}
\right)
-\Gamma_{\alpha\beta}^\eta \Gamma_{\delta\eta\gamma}
+\Gamma_{\alpha\gamma}^\eta \Gamma_{\delta\eta\beta} \tag{3.13}
$$

Riemann记号满足 $R_{\delta\alpha\beta\gamma}=R_{\beta\gamma\delta\alpha}=-R_{\alpha\delta\beta\gamma}=-R_{\delta\alpha\gamma\beta}$，
而$(3.11)$的右边也满足同样的对称性质，故$(3.11)$中只有一个独立方程。

$$
R_{1212}=-\big(b_{11}b_{22}-(b_{12})^2\big) \tag{3.14}
$$

当式子$(3.8)$中$\beta=\gamma$时 Codazzi方程只有两个独立方程

$$
\begin{cases}
\displaystyle\frac{\partial b_{11}}{\partial u^2}-\frac{\partial b_{12}}{\partial u^1}
= b_{1\varepsilon}\Gamma_{12}^\varepsilon -b_{2\varepsilon}\Gamma_{11}^\varepsilon \\[6pt]
\displaystyle\frac{\partial b_{21}}{\partial u^2}-\frac{\partial b_{22}}{\partial u^1}
= b_{1\varepsilon}\Gamma_{22}^\varepsilon -b_{2\varepsilon}\Gamma_{21}^\varepsilon
\end{cases} \tag{3.15}
$$

若曲面的参数$(u,v)$为正交参考系，
Guass方程为

$$
-\frac{1}{\sqrt{EG}}\left\{
\left(\frac{(\sqrt{G})_u}{\sqrt{E}}\right)_v
+\left(\frac{(\sqrt{E})_v}{\sqrt{G}}\right)_u
\right\}
=\frac{LN-M^2}{EG} \tag{3.16}
$$

Codazzi方程可简化为

$$
\begin{cases}
\displaystyle\left(\frac{L}{\sqrt{E}}\right)_v-\left(\frac{M}{\sqrt{E}}\right)_u
-N\frac{(\sqrt{E})_v}{G}-M\frac{(\sqrt{G})_u}{\sqrt{EG}} =0,\\[8pt]
\displaystyle\left(\frac{N}{\sqrt{G}}\right)_u-\left(\frac{M}{\sqrt{G}}\right)_v
-L\frac{(\sqrt{G})_u}{E}-M\frac{(\sqrt{E})_v}{\sqrt{EG}} =0,
\end{cases} \tag{3.17}
$$
